What's new

Every version, newest first. Plicbox updates itself; Settings → Updates shows which one you have.

0.1.17 23 November 2026

  • Clicking the main window brings it in front of a message you're writing (and of opened messages, events, tasks and contacts); each of these windows has its own taskbar button
  • Double-click an attachment in a new message to open it
  • New messages are written, and sent, at the message text size you set in Settings → Appearance

0.1.16 23 November 2026

  • Plicbox's source code is now public, together with its releases, at github.com/bogdanspn/plicbox (GPL-3.0)
  • Updates now come straight from there (the old address keeps working for older versions)

0.1.15 23 November 2026

  • Empty lists and searches with no results now say why and what to try, with an icon: Clear search, or Search all mail when only part of your mail was searched; empty folders say which kind they are (No unread mail, Nothing flagged, No drafts...)
  • Contacts: when a search finds no one, add them as a new contact in one click, filled in from what you searched
  • Plicbox is now free software under the GNU GPL, version 3; About shows the licence, the third-party licences, and links to bogdanrosu.com and the Plicbox website

0.1.14 21 November 2026

  • Drag and drop in a new message: pictures dropped on the text go into it, other files are attached; files dropped anywhere else in the window are attached, pictures too. A hint while dragging says which
  • Attachments that may not get through are flagged before sending: programs (even zipped), shortcuts, files with macros, password-locked zips, and more than 25 MB altogether
  • After the computer sleeps, the window no longer stays frozen or blank (and minimizing and restoring it always redraws it)

0.1.13 18 November 2026

  • Phishing and malware lists: links and senders on public lists of reported phishing and malware sites are flagged in red, and a rule can move such mail to Junk (Settings → Mail → Phishing and malware; the lists download twice a day and nothing about your mail is sent anywhere)
  • Look-alike senders: a domain one letter off one you write to (or your own), or a known person's name on an address they've never used, is warned about; a first message from an address is noted quietly
  • Zip attachments are judged by what's inside: programs, disguised names and password-protected zips are flagged before you open them
  • Attachments you open or save are marked as from the internet, so Office uses Protected View and your antivirus checks them first

0.1.12 17 November 2026

  • New mail: pick the sending account from New's arrow; Ctrl+N writes from the selected account (or the default one in shared views), and works in every module
  • Forward button beside Reply in the reading pane; the header pills and notices are one size, with "/" between parts
  • A small spinner on a sidebar folder while an action on it runs (mark all as read, empty, rename…)
  • Outlook.com: an account Microsoft won't let send from can still be added to read mail; a separate sending password is now saved
  • Messages default to 15 px text; toolbar buttons and search share one height; a new Operations icon; less padding in the event window
  • Google sign-in uses PKCE and a state check, as Google asks

0.1.11 16 November 2026

Spanish; search back in each module's toolbar; new mail you're reading stays put until you leave it, even if a rule files it; tasks understand Spanish dates

0.1.10 16 November 2026

  • Search sits in the title bar in every module, beside the window buttons; the title bar is a little taller to give it room
  • Favorites → Trash for every account, with Empty All Trash; mail can be dropped on the folded sidebar
  • Trash and Outbox say whose mail each row is; the reading pane names the account in mixed views
  • Settings: pages use the window's width; bigger Reading pane pictures; Offline mail shown as a small table with a bar
  • Shorter rows in the Senders lists and Ignored paths
  • Outlook.com: says plainly when Microsoft doesn't let mail apps send from a new mailbox yet

0.1.9 14 November 2026

  • Reading pane at the bottom, on the right, filling the screen, or only in its own window (Settings → Appearance)
  • Font and text size for plain-text mail (Settings → Appearance → Message text)
  • About 50 MB smaller download and install
  • Calendar: today shown like the selected tab; day headers on their own band; agenda with a date block per day
  • Contacts: the card and the In touch tiles use the full width; no Email or Meeting for a contact without an address
  • Tasks: rows line up with the add field
  • Sign-in pages in the browser look like Plicbox, and say so when a sign-in didn't finish
  • Outlook.com: clearer help when mail apps (IMAP) aren't allowed yet; sending through smtp-mail.outlook.com

0.1.8 13 November 2026

  • Folded sidebar: icons line up with the Mail, Calendar, Contacts and Tasks tabs below them, with the same hover and selected look
  • Folded sidebar: accounts and folders that don't fit go under a ⋯ button, with a dot when one of them has new mail
  • About Plicbox: a shorter description

0.1.7 13 November 2026

  • One Outbox under Favorites, always shown, for all accounts
  • Undo send can wait up to 5 minutes (30 seconds by default)
  • Settings → Updates shows how far a download is
  • Thin divider lines between the panes instead of grey gaps
  • Mail sidebar: collapse arrows on the right; Calendar, Contacts and Tasks line up with Mail
  • Calendar: Up next at the bottom of the sidebar, above the month
  • Tasks: account pictures in the folded sidebar; Today / Tomorrow / Next week as proper pills
  • Mail header: shorter, Reply next to the labels, notices on their own row
  • The message notices setting applies at once; the link bar at the bottom of a message lines up again

0.1.6 12 November 2026

  • Outbox: Send always works, also offline; messages wait there and go as soon as the server can be reached
  • Undo send (a few seconds after Send, set in Settings → Mail) and Send later (next to Send)
  • Drafts are saved on this computer first, so nothing is lost offline or in a crash
  • Google calendar and contact changes made offline are kept and sent later
  • Settings → Backup: a daily backup of what's only on this computer (calendars, contacts, tasks, settings), and restore
  • Calendars: import and export .ics, and "Add to Calendar" on invitation attachments; contacts: import and export .vcf
  • Offline mail on by default for new accounts (last year), chosen per account; space used shown in Settings → Mail

0.1.5 12 November 2026

  • Calendars on this computer: use the calendar without a Google account, or next to one (New → Calendar → On this computer). Repeating events, single-occurrence changes, reminders and search all work.
  • Folded sidebar: the account folder menu shows unread counts, and "More" adds up its folders
  • Contacts: the contact card keeps the same padding when the sidebar is folded

0.1.4 11 November 2026

  • Empty Trash and Empty the junk folder show a trashcan with a small ✕, instead of a bare ✕

0.1.3 10 November 2026

  • About Plicbox in the menu: what Plicbox is, its version, and what it's built with
  • Stays sharp on every display: no more blurry window when the display scaling changes after Plicbox started
  • Calendar without a Google account says why it's empty and offers to add one; Quick add no longer fails there
  • Zoho Mail setup mentions the app-specific password needed with two-factor sign-in, and links to your Zoho region
  • Accounts: the button that removes an alias is no longer cut off

0.1.2 31 October 2026

  • Microsoft sign-in and smoother account setup
  • Messages damaged by a crash or power cut download again instead of showing an error
  • 'Sign in again' is now offered right next to a sign-in error, and on top of Accounts → General

Fixes and small improvements.

The first version.
