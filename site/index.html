Free and open

No subscription and no account to make. The source code is public, under the GPL.

Your mail stays on your PC

Stored locally and synced straight with your mail servers. Works offline.

Works with what you have

Gmail, Google Workspace, Outlook.com and any IMAP account, side by side.

The rest of your day, too.

Calendar, contacts and tasks sit one click from your inbox, synced with the same accounts.

Day, week, month and agenda views. Drag to move, invitations answered in one click.

Fast search

Full-text search over all your mail, right on your PC, online or off.

Trackers blocked

Remote pictures and tracking pixels stay off until you choose to show them.

Phishing warnings

Forged senders, look-alike domains and disguised attachments are called out plainly.

Rules

Sort new mail automatically, or create a rule from any message in one click.

Push mail

New mail arrives the moment it lands, with notifications from the tray.

Undo send, send later

Mail waits 30 seconds before it goes, so a slip can be taken back. Or pick when it should go.

Offline, for real

A year of mail to read on the plane. What you send or change meanwhile goes out once you're back.

Daily backups

Calendars, contacts, accounts and rules saved once a day to OneDrive. Import and export .ics and .vcf files.

In your language

English, German, Romanian and Spanish, switched in Settings. Light or dark like Windows, with your own accent colour and text size.
